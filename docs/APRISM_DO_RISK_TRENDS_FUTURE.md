# DO risk history: period-cutoff snapshots

Product Owner decision on 2026-10-18: a historical risk point means the attendance-derived risk tier **using qualifying meetings through the Academic Period end date**. It does not claim what APRISM knew at that wall-clock moment. Current tiers remain live; grades and referrals do not change the frozen attendance thresholds.

`database/migrations/20261018_create_risk_period_snapshots.sql` stores one immutable capture per Academic Period and Operational Class, then one row per eligible Student Class Enrollment. The capture includes its cutoff, generation time, origin, and cohort size. Student rows retain the Tier or no Tier plus the evaluator's attendance counts. The unique period/class and capture/SCE keys prevent duplicate attempts. Historical queries join the stored class and period context; they never reparse Section labels or replace Student/SAE/SCE history.

After a period ends, the first authenticated DO Dashboard request captures classes created by that cutoff. The service uses the same Tier evaluator as live risk with attendance filtered by `attendance_sessions.meeting_date <= period.end_date`. Captured rows are not refreshed by ordinary requests. Periods ending before the migration date are **not automatically backfilled**. If a captured cohort later requires correction, APRISM needs an explicit audited correction policy; no editing UI or silent replacement exists.

The controlled APRISM_QA_DEMO_V1 loader explicitly reconstructs only its own class for completed Prelim and Midterm. Those captures carry `FixtureReconstruction` provenance and are removable by the targeted cleanup. On canonical localhost, the two other eligible College class attempts have no approved retrospective capture, so the chart identifies the QA points as **partial coverage (14 of 16 class attempts)**. Missing and future periods remain unavailable rather than zero. SHS has no configured period history in this local setup.
